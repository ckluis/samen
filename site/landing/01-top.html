The hard 80% of a B2B SaaS. Solved once. Proven on every commit.

Samen is a foundry for launching many B2B products from one substrate. Customer data is masked by default, support sees it only through a second person's approval, erasure destroys keys instead of rows, and the AI agent can only propose. One command emits a running product that passes its own gate. Every guarantee ships with a sabotage that proves its test can fail.

Every B2B product rebuilds the same 80%. And the same leak with it.

Auth, billing, a CRM, a support desk, files, search, exports, an admin tool for your own staff. Every one of those surfaces is also a door onto customer data, each with its own idea of who may open it. Samen builds the 80% once, at the substrate, and makes the safe answer the default value of a field, not a feature someone remembers to add. Then it tries to break every promise it makes.
